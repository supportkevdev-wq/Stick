Stick — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026
Stick collects no personal data of any kind.

Data collection

Stick is a fully offline single-player game. It does not require an internet connection to play, does not create user accounts, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, or gameplay data to any server. Everything - your score, settings, and progress - stays on your device only, for the current session.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other device permission beyond what is needed to run and play sound.

Third-party services

This version of the app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If that changes in a future update (for example, adding ads), this policy will be updated in advance of that release and the update noted above.

Children's privacy

Because no data is collected from any user, this app does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: supportkevdev@gmail.com
